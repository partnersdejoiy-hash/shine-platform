# Part 01 — Vision, Positioning & Business Goals

**Status:** Skeleton · **Depends on:** Part 00 · **Last updated:** 2026-08-05

## 1. Purpose

Define WHY SHINE exists, who it serves, how it is positioned against the
competition, and the business outcomes it must deliver.

## 2. Detailed outline

### 2.1 Vision
- The one-sentence product vision and the "one platform for every conversation" thesis.

### 2.2 Problem statement
- Cost and sprawl of today's stack (chat + meetings + calendar + files + AI + compliance).

### 2.3 Target market & personas
- Personas: Super Admin, Administrator, IT, President, Director, OM, TL, Employee;
  plus buyers (CIO/CTO, IT Ops) and end users.

### 2.4 Competitive positioning
- Positioning map vs Amazon Chime, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp
  Business, Telegram, Google Meet, Zoom, Cisco Webex.
- Differentiators: unified platform, enterprise governance, self-hosted option.

### 2.5 Business goals & success metrics
- Adoption, retention, revenue, enterprise expansion metrics (DAU/MAU, orgs,
  seats, meeting minutes, NRR).

### 2.6 Pricing & packaging summary (detail in Part 21)
- Team / Business / Enterprise tiers; Razorpay billing.

## 3. Key decisions locked so far

- All-in-one enterprise communication ecosystem (messaging, calls, meetings,
  files, tasks, AI, governance).
- Competing at Amazon Chime / Teams / Slack / Zoom parity.
- Primary org DEJOIY India Private Limited; Super Admin creates additional orgs.
- Self-hosted deployment is a first-class requirement (see Part 22).
