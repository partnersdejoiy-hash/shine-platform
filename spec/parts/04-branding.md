# Part 04 — Branding Guidelines

**Status:** Skeleton · **Depends on:** Part 03 · **Last updated:** 2026-08-05

## 1. Purpose

Codify the SHINE / DEJOIY brand — identity, voice, and how branding is applied
in-product, in email, and in self-hosted deployments.

## 2. Detailed outline

### 2.1 Brand identity
- Brand name, tagline, positioning, brand promise.
- Logo suite (ShineLogo), wordmark, iconography, safe space, misuse rules.

### 2.2 Color & typography
- DEJOIY palette usage ratios; typography stack; typographic scale in product vs marketing.

### 2.3 Voice & tone
- Writing principles, terminology, tone in UI vs help vs legal.

### 2.4 In-product branding
- Logo placement, app shell, loading screens, empty states, email footer:
  "Powered by SHINE · An Enterprise Collaboration Platform · Developed by DEJOIY
  India Private Limited".

### 2.5 White-label & per-org branding
- Per-organization logo, colors, signature, support email; default DEJOIY brand.

### 2.6 Policy & footer links
- Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Acceptable Use, Code of Conduct, Data
  Protection, Information Security, Cookie, Retention, Organization Handbook,
  IT Usage, Meeting Recording, Employee Communication policies.

## 3. Key decisions locked so far

- Email shell: dark `#0B0B0B` + gold `#D4AF37` with DEJOIY footer.
- 22 sender identities under `@shine.dejoiy.com`.
- Footer policy links open enterprise modals (no redirects) — planned UI.
